# MALG

**Turn market research into better business conversations.**

MALG is an AI-powered research platform that helps identify promising markets, relevant
companies, and opportunities for AI projects. It brings scattered public information into
structured, source-backed research—giving teams the context to decide where to focus and
what to explore next.

- **Find the right fit.** Connect market signals with company profiles and relevant people.
- **Make opportunities concrete.** Develop project ideas with a proposed scope, deliverables,
  and explicit assumptions for review.
- **Keep research usable.** Bring findings into Twenty CRM, with sources and knowledge gaps
  visible alongside the results.

Specialized AI agents handle research within defined limits. People review the findings,
shape the opportunities, and own the relationships. MALG does not automate outreach.

Self-hosted and actively developed, MALG combines a Python backend, an Angular console,
and durable research history. Currently pre-alpha.
